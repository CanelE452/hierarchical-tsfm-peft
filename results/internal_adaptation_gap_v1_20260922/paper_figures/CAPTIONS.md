# Publication captions / 미팅 설명

## Figure 1. Internal adaptation accuracy gap

**English.** Small open and filled markers denote seeds 92601 and 92602, respectively; short horizontal strokes denote their arithmetic mean. Deterministic F0 references have one marker. Validation-selected LoRA improves over HEAD by 2.172% on ETTm2, and validation-selected LoRA+ by 1.695% on Jena (ratios of seed-mean TEST losses). Saved paired block-bootstrap effect intervals are [1.294%, 3.053%] and [0.601%, 2.795%], respectively; these are not confidence intervals on the raw-score points. No new intervals were computed. The numerical y-axis limits are displayed and do not start at zero. Errors are TRAIN-standard-deviation-scaled twice-pinball losses (lower is better). ETTm2 and Jena are development datasets, not independent external replications. LoRA+ is an existing method, not a method proposed here. The output-only HEAD continued to improve at the maximum training budget, so this comparison does not establish an intrinsic limitation of output adaptation. The reported cost difference is not an equal-quality time-to-target comparison.

**한국어.** 고정된 학습 조건에서 출력 HEAD와 내부 적응의 정확도 차이를 보여준다. 평균과 두 seed를 모두 표시한다. 신뢰구간은 저장된 개선율 구간이며 원점수 오차막대가 아니다. ETTm2 HEAD의 학습이 더 진행되면 격차가 줄 가능성을 배제하지 않는다.

## Figure 2. Quality–cost trade-off

**English.** Small markers show each seed at its own validation-selected checkpoint; large markers show arithmetic means of their costs and TEST errors. F0_LONG is a deterministic reference. Lower-left indicates lower error and lower adaptation cost. Cold-ready costs include the recorded preparation, model loading, charged feature cache, training, validation and checkpoint I/O through selection. F0 uses the saved reference-ready cost, including its reference evaluation. Timing variation is visible in seed points; ETTm2 LoRA+ fits were flagged as noisy in the source receipts. Costs correspond to the selected checkpoints and do not represent equal-quality time-to-target ratios. The reported cost difference is not an equal-quality time-to-target comparison. Errors are TRAIN-standard-deviation-scaled twice-pinball losses (lower is better). ETTm2 and Jena are development datasets, not independent external replications. LoRA+ is an existing method, not a method proposed here. The output-only HEAD continued to improve at the maximum training budget, so this comparison does not establish an intrinsic limitation of output adaptation.

**한국어.** 왼쪽 아래일수록 정확도와 비용 모두 유리하다. 비용은 서로 다른 품질의 선택 체크포인트까지 걸린 값이다. ETTm2 LoRA+의 시간 변동도 숨기지 않는다. 같은 정확도에 도달하는 속도비로 해석하면 안 된다.

## Figure 3. Adaptation trajectories

**English.** Each thin line follows the recorded validation checkpoints of one method and seed: (a,b) ETTm2 and (c,d) Jena, against optimizer updates and actual cold-ready cost. Method is encoded by color, marker and line style; open/filled markers distinguish the two seeds. No time coordinates were averaged or interpolated into new observations. In Jena, internal methods reached their selected validation checkpoints near 64–128 updates; later updates degraded validation performance in this fixed run. This observation does not identify the cause. The output-only HEAD continued to improve at the maximum training budget, so this comparison does not establish an intrinsic limitation of output adaptation. The Jena legacy TIME_TO_TARGET threshold, based on degraded final-step internal performance, is not used as adaptation-acceleration evidence. The reported cost difference is not an equal-quality time-to-target comparison. Errors are TRAIN-standard-deviation-scaled twice-pinball losses (lower is better). ETTm2 and Jena are development datasets, not independent external replications. LoRA+ is an existing method, not a method proposed here.

**한국어.** Jena 내부 적응은 64–128 step의 선택 시점 이후 검증 성능이 악화했다. 이것만으로 과적합의 원인을 입증하지 않는다. ETTm2 HEAD는 512 step에서도 개선 중이다. 시간축은 seed별 실제 기록이며 느슨해진 기존 목표 도달 표는 가속 증거로 사용하지 않는다.

Means are taken across the two seeds before computing the ratio. Internal methods were selected on validation: LoRA for ETTm2 and LoRA+ for Jena. Positive values favor internal adaptation; negative values favor HEAD. All channels are retained in source order, and no channel-level confidence intervals are invented. Errors are TRAIN-standard-deviation-scaled twice-pinball losses (lower is better). ETTm2 and Jena are development datasets, not independent external replications. LoRA+ is an existing method, not a method proposed here. The output-only HEAD continued to improve at the maximum training budget, so this comparison does not establish an intrinsic limitation of output adaptation. The reported cost difference is not an equal-quality time-to-target comparison.

**한국어.** 채널별 두 seed 평균 원점수의 비율을 사용했다. 양수와 음수 채널을 모두 남겨 전체 평균 개선을 모든 채널의 개선으로 오해하지 않도록 했다.

## Figure 6. Context length control

**English.** F0_SHORT uses four days of history and is a context-length diagnostic. F0_LONG and all adaptation arms use the same eight-day context. The internal comparator is validation-selected LoRA on ETTm2 and LoRA+ on Jena. Small open and filled markers denote seeds 92601 and 92602, respectively; short horizontal strokes denote their arithmetic mean. Deterministic F0 references have one marker. The comparison controls this specific context-length alternative rather than establishing an intrinsic requirement for internal adaptation. Errors are TRAIN-standard-deviation-scaled twice-pinball losses (lower is better). ETTm2 and Jena are development datasets, not independent external replications. LoRA+ is an existing method, not a method proposed here. The output-only HEAD continued to improve at the maximum training budget, so this comparison does not establish an intrinsic limitation of output adaptation. The reported cost difference is not an equal-quality time-to-target comparison.

**한국어.** HEAD와 내부 적응은 모두 같은 8일 입력을 받는다. 4일 F0는 문맥 길이 진단이며 주 비교의 유리한 기준선으로 바꾸지 않았다. 관찰된 내부 적응 이득을 단순한 입력 길이 차이와 구분하는 보조 그림이다.
