# Fixed A: Q contribution and the limits of a no-Q equivalence claim

The method is unchanged from v11. For the fixed TRAIN PCA basis U, P=UUᵀ and Q=I−P, the selected latent LoRA predicts bφ(X)=Fφ(XU)Uᵀ. The frozen, previously trained LEVEL branch is q*(X)=repeat(last(XQ))+G*(XQ−repeat(last(XQ))). G* is the shared, bias-free linear temporal composition 512→32→48. The full predictor is bφ+q*. Only the original v11 LoRA was adapted; v12 performs no new parameter fitting in the completed old-data scope.

The new output controls hold bφ fixed. MAIN_ONLY returns bφ, MAIN_LAST adds the last observed context residual, and A_FULL adds the full learned q*. FULL−MAIN measures the entire Q contribution; FULL−LAST isolates the additional learned temporal correction above residual-level persistence. MAIN_ONLY is constrained to P, so its large loss is not evidence against every independently trained compressed forecaster. The actual parent Q was reconstructed without loading the TSFM and agreed with the saved paired full/main outputs at the fixed tolerance.

On exposed Robin/Jena/Hog development periods, A_FULL reduced MSE and MAE relative to MAIN_LAST as well as MAIN_ONLY. The full-minus-last pooled MSE differences were −0.073841, −0.031360 and −0.465357. Conditional block intervals exclude zero for these pooled contrasts. The intervals condition on the already selected models and periods; they do not include repeated model selection or establish new-population generalization. Per-period, per-seed and channel results remain in the machine-readable outputs. External counterexamples are retained: uncompressed MSE-LoRA has lower pooled MSE on all three tasks, and native LoRA has substantially lower MAE.

For exact orthogonal P/Q and a shared linear temporal G, q* remains in Q. Under fully observed, uniformly weighted squared loss, the full and main-only losses differ by a φ-independent term. Under the actual masked channel-macro objective, however, their gradient difference is 2 J_bᵀ W q*, with w_tc=m_tc/(|V| n_c). A sufficient structural condition is P̄WQ̄=0; ordinary Euclidean orthogonality alone is insufficient. The 24 bounded backward pairs exhibited very small FP32 differences on complete batches and nonzero differences beyond the fixed diagnostic tolerance on missing-target batches. Every TRAIN contract contains at least some missing target occurrences.

An identical instantaneous gradient also does not guarantee identical training policy. Relative-threshold ReduceLROnPlateau is not invariant to arbitrary additive loss offsets. The scalar replay matched all twelve actual v11 A learning-rate histories. On Robin/Hog, the actual fixed-Q/VAL conditional offset did not change the replayed schedules; Jena's missing VAL targets precluded that constant-offset interpretation. A synthetic counterexample demonstrates the possibility, not an observed scheduler divergence in these actual runs.

The old-data no-Q refit is therefore omitted under the authorized same-checkpoint-claim branch, not declared mathematically redundant. Independently optimized no-Q superiority and whole-trajectory equivalence remain untested. This distinction is explicit in NO_Q_RUN_DECISION.json and must survive manuscript use.

P/Q error energies use a common complete-target-vector subset. They are not additive channelwise errors or an additive MAE decomposition. Hog's subset covers about 84.17% of target vectors and reverses A/MSE-LoRA's ordering relative to the primary masked metric. It cannot replace the primary evaluation or identify a unique cause of the remaining error.
